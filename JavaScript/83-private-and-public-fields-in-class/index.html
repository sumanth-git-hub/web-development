Private and Public Fields in Class

Private methods are denoted by the # prefix in their names. They can only be accessed within the class that defines them and are not accessible in subclasses or from external code.

In FY'24, the government collected Rs 8,263 crore from GST on health insurance premiums, with an additional Rs 1,484 crore from health reinsurance premiums. Compared to previous years, this amount shows a steady increase from Rs 7,638 crore in FY23 and Rs 5,354 crore in FY22 for health insurance premiums. Most health insurance plans are subject to the 18% GST. However, this tax does not affect plans for society's weaker and differently-abled sections. Thus, the Ministry of Finance is being requested to lower or eliminate the GST on health and life insurance.
